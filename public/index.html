    Why dew point, not humidity?

    Humidity is a percentage that changes with temperature, so 80% on a cool morning can feel totally fine. Dew point measures how much water is actually in the air. Hair is made of keratin, which grabs that water and swells unevenly, and that's frizz. Under about 45°F dew point, most hair behaves. Over 60°F, frizz-prone hair starts misbehaving. Over 65°F, it's soup. Very dry air (under about 35°F) causes static and dries out curls.
